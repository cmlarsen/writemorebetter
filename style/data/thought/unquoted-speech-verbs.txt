# Verbs that tag unquoted direct speech ("I'm leaving, he said."). Project-authored for
# writemorebetter (#353), MIT OR Apache-2.0. A subset of dialogue/speech-verbs.txt: only verbs that
# report words. Sound and action verbs (cry, howl, bark, roar, sob, groan, moan, sing, echo, boom,
# scream) and verbs that also name a non-speech act (read, continue, agree, admit, return, observe,
# call) are left out, because without quote marks "I looked away, the baby cried." and "I said
# nothing, he continued." read as narration. One lemma per line.
